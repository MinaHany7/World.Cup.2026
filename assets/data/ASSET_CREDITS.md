# Asset credits

- **Player portraits:** see [`photo-attribution.csv`](photo-attribution.csv) for each file’s creator, source page, licence, licence URL, credit and permission note. The 197 compact WebP thumbnails are embedded in `assets/data/tournament-data.js`; six featured scouting cards also use higher-resolution local derivatives, listed in the same CSV. Preserve the CSV and follow each image’s specific licence, including any CC BY / CC BY-SA attribution or share-alike terms.
- **National flags:** see [`flag-sources.csv`](flag-sources.csv); flags are stored locally under `assets/flags/` and sourced from FlagCDN.
- **Walkout, stadium, and trophy artwork:** `assets/img/stadium-walkout.webp`, `assets/img/walkout-player.webp`, and `assets/img/trophy-and-ball.webp` are original AI-generated illustrations made for this portfolio. The player is a fictional silhouette; the cup is a generic illustration, not the official FIFA trophy and not a record of a real match.
- **Fonts:** Inter and Barlow Condensed are locally hosted Google Fonts, released under the SIL Open Font License. Licence texts are included in `assets/fonts/`.
- The PBIX, workbook, video, and CV are materials supplied by Mina Hany Wahba. The site is an independent portfolio project, not an official FIFA product.
