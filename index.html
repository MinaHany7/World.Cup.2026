Sources & methodology +

All tournament totals are recalculated from the supplied workbook. Attendance is reported attendance, not a ticket audit. The player list is a scouting watchlist, not a complete roster or a transfer recommendation.

The workbook has no player minutes, expected goals, progressive actions, contract, salary, injury or transfer-fee data. Per-90 comparisons and valuations are therefore not presented.
